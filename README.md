# MegaBlog

A full-featured blog platform built with **React 19**, **TypeScript**, and **Vite**.

Sign up, write, and publish — or keep your drafts. MegaBlog gives every user their own corner of the web to create and share posts with rich formatting and custom imagery.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 **Authentication** — sign up, log in, and log out with session management
- ✍️ **Rich content editing** — write posts with a full-featured TinyMCE editor
- 🖼️ **Featured images** — upload and manage a cover image for every post
- 📝 **Full post management** — create, edit, and delete your own posts
- 🕐 **Draft support** — save posts as active or inactive
- 📚 **Browsable archive** — browse all published posts and view individual post pages
- 🌙 **Dark/light theme** — persistent theme toggle across the app
- 📱 **Responsive design** — mobile-friendly header and menu
- ✨ **Scroll-reveal animations** — subtle motion as content comes into view
